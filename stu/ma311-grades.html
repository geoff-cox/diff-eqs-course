    Section 1.2 Grades & Coursework

    Subsection Final Grades

    Your final grade is determined by a combination of the following items:
    • Number of Core Topic Passes (# of Ps)
      🔗
    • Number of Secondary Topic Passes (# of Ps)
      🔗
    • Overall Homework Score (%)
      🔗
    • Final Exam Score (%)
      🔗
    The table below shows that the letter grade you will receive will come from the first row where EVERY requirement is met.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Homework

    Reading Assignments (35%).

    Each section of the textbook includes some reading questions that must be completed through Runestone. These are straightforward questions designed to reinforce your understanding of the key concepts.
    🔗
    🔗

    Online Exercises (65%).

    Each topic also includes a set of online exercises that must be completed in WeBWorK. Most problems require step-by-step work, so you should complete them on paper before entering final answers online. Problems may allow multiple attempts; if you struggle, use it as a chance to learn and ask for help before using all of your attempts.
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Opportunities to Demonstrate Proficiency (ODPs)

    Instead of traditional quizzes or tests, you will complete ODPs, which are short assessments scored on a P or N scale:
    • P: Proficient — you have demonstrated mastery of the topic.
      🔗
    • N: Not yet proficient — you need more practice with the topic.
      🔗
    🔗
    ODPs focus on specific course topics. Some are completed in class, while others are assigned outside of class. Because proficiency is about eventual mastery, you will have multiple opportunities to pass each topic.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Final Exam

    You will take a traditional final exam that is points-based and covers all topics from the course. You will also have one final opportunity to demonstrate proficiency in one or more core topics. While the exam may include secondary topics, those questions will not count toward secondary passes.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection Secondary(S) Topic Pass ➜ 1% Homework

    You can trade in one Secondary Topic P for a flat 1% increase to your homework grade. Here are the rules:
    • To trade in a P, email me with the particular S-topic you want to trade in.
      🔗
    • After you trade it in, you lose the P for that S-topic.
      🔗
    • You can repass an S-topic that you have traded in.
      🔗
    • You may not trade in more than one P for the same S-topic.
      🔗
    • You must submit your trade-in request before the final exam.
      🔗
    🔗
